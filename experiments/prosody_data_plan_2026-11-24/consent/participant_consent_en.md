## The study
Voise is a graduation project on voice cloning in Jordanian Arabic. This study measures how a person's speaking
style (pitch, rhythm, pauses, emphasis) varies between recording sessions.

## What I will do
- 3 recording sessions on different days, about 20-25 minutes each.
- Each session: 20 fixed sentences read aloud, free talk on a topic, questions and answers, and retelling an event.
- Sessions may use my phone or a recorder the researcher brings, in different rooms.

## Storage
- On the project machine only, encrypted (AES-256).
- Never uploaded to a cloud service or website, never published, never shared.
- Labelled by code, not by name.
- Deleted when the project ends, or earlier on request.

## Always prohibited
- Impersonating me or attributing words to me that I did not say.
- Publishing or sharing my voice, or a cloned voice made from it.
- Any use outside what I tick below.

## Consent (each item separate)
- [ ] **(required)** Recording my voice in 3 sessions and measuring my speaking-style features for this study.
- [ ] **(required)** Logging session information: device, room, date, sex and age band.
- [ ] (optional) Using my recordings in **voice-cloning experiments** within Voise only. This includes generating

- [ ] (optional) Using my recordings to **train** the Voise model.
- [ ] (optional) Letting other participants in this study listen to clips of my voice in an internal listening
  test, without receiving copies.

## Withdrawal
- I can withdraw at any time, without giving a reason, and without any consequence.
- On withdrawal, my recordings, the features extracted from them, and any cloned clips or models trained on my
  voice are deleted, and aggregate figures are recomputed without me.
- I receive written confirmation of deletion within 7 days.
